## How staff use it

1. Verify the customer and shipping assignment using the existing onboarding controls. Receive/process a package against operational evidence.
2. Under **Billing & collections**, prepare an invoice with its exact package UUID, measured weight and every assessed extra, including verified zero where no charge is due. Put the complete assessment reference in the internal evidence field. Above 10 lb, the owner must approve a matching-weight quote first.
3. Review the draft, then issue it with a customer explanation and internal reason. The customer sees their issued invoice and collection history; draft invoices and internal proof/cost records remain restricted.
4. Verify actual funds before entering a receipt. Use a unique receipt/transaction reference and verification evidence, not bank-account/card details. Partial collections reduce the outstanding balance. If a response is lost, refresh and inspect the ledger before retrying.
5. Correct a payment using an appended reversal, and a charge using a debit or owner-approved credit. Credits cannot exceed a charge category or reduce charges below net collections. A credit does not send a cash refund. Disputes prevent release until resolved.
6. Record attributable actual costs, allocation/FX evidence and explicit unknowns. A zero asserts the category was assessed as no cost; a blank means unknown. Billed contribution is not net business profit and is separate from collections.
7. Under **Consolidations**, create a unique draft reference. Add verified received/processing package UUIDs, record the verified partner/AWB reference, and dispatch the manifest. Continue recording each package's actual checkpoints independently. Confirm batch arrival; close only after its packages have terminal outcomes.
8. Clear actual document/customs requirements and verify settlement before recording readiness/handover evidence. No scanner is connected; unscanned uploads remain quarantined and cannot be approved. Waiver is a documented authorized decision based on independently verified alternatives, not a clean scan.
