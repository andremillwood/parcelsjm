## 1. Product purpose

Parcels JA helps Jamaican shoppers prepare and follow US purchases through an account for pre-alerts, package records and requested documents.

## 2. Primary user

A Jamaican online shopper or small-business owner, usually browsing on a phone, deciding whether to explore registration and understand the shipping process.

## 3. Principles

1. Earn trust before asking for details: qualify availability beside acquisition actions and show only implemented account features.
2. Make the purchase feel personal: preserve the approved Jamaican home and business imagery and original logo.
3. One clear next step beats several equal offers: registration exploration is primary; the customer demo supplies supporting evidence.
4. Let the content carry the brand: navy provides structure, yellow directs attention, and labels appear only when they add information.
5. A visitor can browse comfortably on a phone: readable text, large controls, no motion dependency and no horizontal scrolling.
